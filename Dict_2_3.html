Be-verbs 補充提示 (Grammar Note):

動詞短語中的 be 需要根據人稱與時態作相應變化：

Present (現在式): am / is / are
Past (過去式): was / were
• He / She / It ➔ is / was
• You / We / They ➔ are / were
• I ➔ am / was

Unit 2 Focus

c. Friends Actions & Language Focus

What SHOULD a good friend do?

  • share things with one another
  • share feelings with one another
  • help one another
  • support / listen to one another
  • take care of one another
  • get on with one another

What SHOULDN'T a good friend do?

  • be mean to one another
  • argue with one another
  • fight with one another
  • lie to one another
  • laugh at one another
  • shout at one another

Language Focus: Each other vs. One another

each other

用來指 兩個人 (two people)

e.g. Mark and Sarah are friends. They shouldn't quarrel with each other.

one another

用來指 多於兩個人 (more than two people)

e.g. Friends shouldn't be jealous of one another.
