      Mark and Sarah are friends. They shouldn't quarrel with each other.
      Pattern 2

      Henry shares his toys with his friends.
      重要文法規則: each other vs. one another
      each other

      專門用於指代 2 個人 (TWO people)。

      例: Mark and Sarah (2人) shouldn't quarrel with each other.
      one another

      專門用於指代 多於 2 個人 (MORE THAN TWO people)。

      例: Good friends should help one another.
